# Research Dossier: ARTICLE-08 (أفضل صبغ خارجي في الكويت يتحمل حرارة 50+ والغبار)

## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Diagnosis
- **Search Intent**: Comparative & Commercial Investigation. Villa and building owners in Kuwait looking for the most durable exterior wall coatings capable of withstanding extreme environmental stressors:
  - Ambient summer temperatures reaching 50°C–54°C in July/August.
  - Wall surface temperatures on south and west exposures exceeding 70°C.
  - High UV radiation causing rapid polymer degradation and color fading.
  - Frequent dust storms (السافي / طوز) causing physical abrasion and electrostatic dust accumulation.
- **Top Competitors Compared**:
  1. **Jotun Jotashield Colour Extreme / Cool Shelter**: Market leader in Kuwait. Utilizes infrared-reflective pigments and low dirt pick-up binders.
  2. **Caparol ThermoSan NQG (Nano-Quartz Grid)**: Premium German technology. Best self-cleaning properties against Kuwait dust; inorganic nano-quartz grid prevents dirt particles from adhering.
  3. **Hempel Concrete Shield / Sigma Tex**: Excellent mechanical hardness, abrasion resistance against desert winds, and economic value.

## 2. Technical Evaluation Criteria in Kuwait
- **Solar Reflectance Index (SRI)**: Higher SRI reduces thermal transmission into indoor walls, saving AC power.
- **Low Dirt Pick-up (مقاومة السافي)**: Paint film must be cross-linked hard enough not to soften under 60°C wall heat, preventing dust from sinking into the binder.
- **Elastomeric Crack-Bridging (مرونة التغطية)**: Must bridge hairline settling and thermal cracks (up to 1.5mm) caused by thermal expansion cycles.
- **Vapor Permeability (sd value)**: Must allow underlying masonry moisture to escape while blocking exterior liquid water.

## 3. Recommended Color Palette for Kuwait Facades
- Light tones with high SRI: **Off-White (أوف وايت)**, **Desert Sand (رملي فاتح)**, **Warm Greige (بيج رمادي دافئ)**.
- Dark colors (كحلي، بني محروق، رمادي غامق) should only be used as minor accent trims because they absorb over 85% of solar radiation and accelerate heat transfer.
