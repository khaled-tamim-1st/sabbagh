# Research Dossier: ARTICLE-12 (طريقة الصبغ فوق الصبغ الزيتي القديم بدون تقشير)

## 1. Executive Summary & Chemical Dynamics
- **Search Intent**: How-To & Procedural / Problem-Solving. Homeowners renovating older Kuwaiti homes (built 1985–2010 in areas like Rawda, Yarmouk, Rumaithiya, Bayan, Farwaniya) seeking to replace old yellowing oil-based gloss enamel with contemporary ultra-matte or eggshell water-based paints (Jotun Fenomastic My Home / Pure Colors).
- **The Core Conflict (Oil vs Water)**:
  - Alkyd oil paints dry by oxidative cross-linking, creating a dense, non-porous, hydrophobic vitrified surface.
  - Acrylic water-based emulsions cannot wet or adhere to glossy oil films. Without mechanical scuffing and chemical bonding agents, the new paint will peel off in rubbery sheets within weeks.
  - Applying conventional gypsum wall putty directly over glossy oil causes catastrophic delamination.

## 2. Definitive Field Diagnostics: The Solvent Wipe Test
- How to confirm whether existing paint is oil-based:
  1. Dampen a rag with pure Acetone or mineral spirits.
  2. Rub vigorously on an inconspicuous test patch for 30 seconds.
  3. **Result Interpretation**:
     - *Paint softens, dissolves, or rubs off onto cloth* = Water-based acrylic (can be repainted directly after cleaning).
     - *Paint remains rock-hard, glossy, and leaves zero color on cloth* = Oil-based alkyd (requires complete transition protocol).

## 3. The 5-Stage Transition Engineering Protocol
1. **Degreasing & De-waxing**: Wash with Trisodium Phosphate (TSP) substitute or citrus degreaser to remove 15+ years of hand oils, dust, and incense (بخور) residue.
2. **Mechanical Scuff-Sanding (كسر اللمعة)**: Sand thoroughly with 120–150 grit sandpaper until the surface turns completely cloudy matte; this creates mechanical micro-grooves for grip.
3. **Cracked Putty Treatment**: V-cut cracks, apply fiberglass mesh tape, and patch with flexible acrylic putty (never brittle gypsum).
4. **Super-Adhesion Bonding Primer (البرايمر الرابط / Tie-Coat)**: Apply 1 full coat of water-based high-adhesion universal primer (such as Caparol Haftgrund or Jotun Fenomastic Primer). This bonds to the scuffed oil below and presents a receptive mineral tooth above.
5. **Finishing Water-Based Topcoats**: 2 coats of premium water-based paint (Jotun Fenomastic Pure Color / Rich Matt).
