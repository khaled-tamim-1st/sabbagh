# Research Dossier: ARTICLE-01
**Topic**: Cost of painting a room or apartment in Kuwait (كم تكلفة صبغ الغرفة والشقة في الكويت)
**Primary Keyword**: سعر صبغ الغرفة بالكويت
**Date**: October 2026

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Market Landscape
In Kuwait's residential finishing market, the cost of painting is calculated either **per room (بالغرفة)**, **per square meter (بالمتر المربع)**, or as a **lump-sum package for an entire apartment/floor (مقاولة شقة كاملة)**.
Homeowners frequently struggle with massive pricing discrepancies: a freelance painter may quote 15 KWD per room, while a reputable contracting team quotes 40–50 KWD.
The difference lies almost entirely in:
1. **Material quality**: Commercial unbranded paints vs genuine Jotun Fenomastic (سطل جوتن الأصلي).
2. **Substrate preparation**: Simple roller wash vs scraping loose paint, applying 2 coats of acrylic putty (معجون), machine sanding with dust vacuuming, and primer.
3. **Protection & Cleanup**: Wrapping parquet/marble floors and luxury furniture in heavy polyethylene nylon and masking tape vs leaving paint splatter on skirting boards (النعلات) and door frames.

## 2. Verified Benchmark Price Ranges (Kuwait Dinar - KWD)
* **Single Standard Room (4×4m to 4×5m)**:
  - *Labor Only (بدون مواد)*: 12 – 18 KWD.
  - *Standard Refresh (صبغ مائي نظيف وجهين + معجون خفيف)*: 20 – 30 KWD.
  - *Full Premium System (Jotun Fenomastic + وجهين معجون + برايمر + حماية شاملة)*: 35 – 50 KWD.
* **Master Bedroom with Dressing Area**: 40 – 65 KWD.
* **Large Reception Hall / Majlis (صالة استقبال 6×8م أو أكبر)**: 50 – 85 KWD.
* **Apartment Packages**:
  - *1-Bedroom / Studio (غرفة وصالة)*: 70 – 120 KWD.
  - *2-Bedroom Apartment (غرفتين وصالة وممر)*: 120 – 200 KWD.
  - *3-Bedroom Apartment (3 غرف وصالة وممر)*: 160 – 280 KWD.
  - *Full Villa Floor (دور فيلا 4-5 غرف وصالتين)*: 300 – 480 KWD.

## 3. Cost Influencers & Hidden Traps
- **Ceiling condition (الأسقف الجبسية)**: Damaged gypsum joints and yellow water stains from AC duct leaks require stain-blocker primer (برايمر زيتي عازل) before water paint, adding 5–10 KWD per ceiling.
- **Color transition**: Shifting from dark shades (e.g. navy, brown) to off-white or beige requires an extra coat of white primer, increasing material consumption.
- **Scam Alert**: Some rogue painters dilute a 30 KWD Jotun drum with 40% water or refill empty Jotun drums with cheap commercial paint (أصباغ مقلدة).

- "Checklist before hiring" ensuring written scope and fixed delivery timeline.
