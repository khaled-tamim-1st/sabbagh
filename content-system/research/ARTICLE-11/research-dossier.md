# Research Dossier: ARTICLE-11 (أفضل صبغ لحمامات ومطابخ الكويت)

## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Diagnosis
- **Search Intent**: Informational & Problem-Solving. Homeowners and tenants in Kuwait struggling with:
  - Peeling, bubbling bathroom ceilings caused by shower steam and lack of window ventilation.
  - Black mold (العفن الأسود) colonies forming in bathroom corners and AC exhaust ducts.
  - Kitchen walls above tile backsplashes absorbing cooking oils, vapor, and turmeric/spice odors, leading to stubborn yellowing and flaking.
- **Why Standard Emulsion Paints Fail**:
  - Standard interior paints (مثل الدهانات المطفية العادية غير القابلة للغسيل) have porous binder matrices. Hot steam penetrates the film, condenses against the cool ceiling substrate, and physically debonds the paint.
  - Cellulose-based fillers in cheap wall putty serve as food for fungal spores in high humidity.

## 2. Specialized Hygiene & Wet-Area Coating Systems
- **Hygiene / Anti-Bacterial Formulations**:
  - *Jotun Fenomastic Hygiene*: Incorporates non-leaching silver ion / micro-biocide technology that destroys fungal cell walls upon contact.
  - *Scrub Resistance Class 1 (ISO 11998)*: Allows aggressive washing with dishwashing detergents and sponges without burnishing or loss of paint film thickness.
- **Recommended Sheen**: **Quarter Sheen (ربع لمعة / Silk)** or **Semi-Gloss (نصف لمعة)**. Matte is strictly prohibited for wet areas because its open micro-texture traps oils and grease.

## 3. Protocol for Treating Existing Black Mold
1. *Never scrape dry mold*: Spores become airborne and inhale into lungs/ducts.
2. *Chemical Fungal Wash*: Spray 1:3 diluted sodium hypochlorite (كلور مخفف) or specialized quaternary ammonium wash (Jotun Fungicidal Wash). Let sit 20 minutes, then wipe clean.
3. *Stain-Blocking Fungicidal Primer*: Prevents dead mold stains from bleeding through new paint.
4. *2 Coats Anti-Mold Topcoat*: Ensure continuous, gap-free seal.
