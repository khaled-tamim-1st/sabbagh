# Research Dossier: ARTICLE-06 (صبغ أبواب خشب في الكويت)

## 1. Executive Summary & Market Verification
- **Target Search Intent**: Commercial & How-To. Users in Kuwait looking to renovate existing interior and exterior wooden doors (أبواب الغرف، المداخل، الحمامات) without replacing expensive door frames or carpentry.
- **Local Context**:
  - Kuwait homes heavily feature solid wood (خشب سويدي أو زان) for main entrances and veneered flush doors (أبواب كبس قشرة سنديان / MDF) for bedrooms and apartments.
  - Due to intense indoor air conditioning alternating with external heat/humidity, doors expand, shrink, and experience peeling paint, yellowing, or edge chipping.
  - Three distinct painting systems dominate the Kuwait carpentry market:
    1. **الصبغ الناري (دوكو / Polyurethane Auto-grade Finish)**: The highest standard for modern villas and apartments. High scratch resistance, smooth touch, satin/matte sheen. Usually sprayed with an air compressor.
    2. **الصبغ الزيتي (Oil-Based Enamel)**: The traditional economical solution. Applied on-site with fine mohair rollers, durable and washable, but prone to yellowing over 2-3 years.
    3. **الصبغ اللاكر الشفاف أو الاستر (Clear Lacquer / Wood Stain)**: Used for natural oak/teak veneer to highlight wood grain patterns.

## 2. Pricing Matrix Verified in Kuwait (2026 Benchmarks)
- **Standard bedroom door refresh (وجهين صبغ ناري فوق القديم مع سنفرة)**: 14 – 20 د.ك للباب الواحد شامل المواد والمصنعية.
- **Full stripping and refinishing (إزالة الصبغ القديم بالكامل حتى الخشب وسحب معجون ناري)**: 22 – 30 د.ك للباب الواحد.
- **Door frame and trims (المجرور والبرواز والنعلة)**: 4 – 8 د.ك للباب.
- **Main entrance villa door (باب خشب زان/سويدي خارجي مقاوم للعوامل الجوية)**: 45 – 85 د.ك حسب النقوش والزجاج وارتفاع الباب.
- **Disassembly and hardware handling (فك المقابض والمفصلات والكوالين)**: 2 – 4 د.ك أو مشمولة في الاتفاق إذا تجاوز عدد الأبواب 5.

## 3. Technical Protocol (Best Practices in Kuwait)
1. **Masking & Hardware Removal**: Remove handles, locksets, hinges; mask gaskets with precision crepe tape.
2. **Surface Degreasing & Sanding**: Sand with grit 120/180 to break glaze; remove body oils and wax near handle areas.
3. **Automotive/Polyester Putty**: Fix dents and deep gouges with 2K polyester wood putty (معجون ناري).
4. **Isolating Primer (عازل بولي يوريثان)**: Seal the wood to stop tannin bleed and prevent paint peeling.
5. **Spray Application (الرش بالكومبريسور)**: 2 to 3 coats of polyurethane/duco paint with grit 400 wet sanding between coats.
6. **Topcoat Protective Clear (توب كوت مطفي أو حريري)**: Locks color and prevents fingerprint marks.

## 4. Key Pitfalls & Contractor Red Flags
- Spraying inside furnished bedrooms without a sealed plastic tent (غبار الصبغ يلتصق بالأثاث والمكيفات).
- Painting over brass hardware without removal, leaving messy splatters on lock cylinders.
- Skipping the primer/isolator, leading to bubbling or bleeding through water/oil stains.
- Thinning lacquer with poor quality recycled thinners (ثنر مغشوش) that cause a hazy bloom or cracking under AC cooling.
