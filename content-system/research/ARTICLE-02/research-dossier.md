# Research Dossier: ARTICLE-02
**Topic**: Sigma exterior coating prices in Kuwait (سعر متر السيجما في الكويت)
**Primary Keyword**: سعر متر السيجما الكويت
**Date**: October 2026

---

## 1. Market Context & Significance
Sigma (السيجما) is the dominant exterior facade finish in Kuwait, adorning over 55% of all private residences and investment properties in areas like Al-Mutlaa, West Abdullah Al-Mubarak, Sabah Al-Ahmad, and South Surra.
Homeowners frequently compare Sigma to natural stone (الحجر الطبيعي) or marble cladding. Stone costs between 12 to 25+ KWD/m² installed, whereas high-grade acrylic Sigma costs **4.5 to 6 KWD/m²** complete, making it the premier cost-effective, weather-resistant solution for Kuwait's severe climate.

## 2. Itemized Cost Structure per Square Meter (m²)
* **Complete Turnkey Package (توريد وتنفيذ شامل)**:
  - *Standard Local/Saudi Sigma*: 4.0 – 4.8 KWD / m².
  - *Premium Brand (Jotun / Hempel / Caparol + Protective Topcoat)*: 5.0 – 6.2 KWD / m².
* **Component Breakdown per m²**:
  - *Scaffolding (توريد وتركيب سقالة حديدية آمنة)*: 0.800 – 1.200 KWD.
  - *Mesh & Corner Beads (شبك فايبر غلاس أو مجلفن + زوايا)*: 0.350 – 0.500 KWD.
  - *Base Primer (برايمر مائي عازل خارجي)*: 0.400 – 0.600 KWD.
  - *Sigma Textured Paste (عجينة السيجما الملونة 1-3 ملم)*: 1.500 – 2.000 KWD.
  - *Protective Acrylic / Polyurethane Topcoat (طبقة حماية ضد الغبار وأشعة UV)*: 0.500 – 0.800 KWD.
  - *Application Labor (أجور اليد العاملة والتشطيب بالمالج)*: 1.500 – 2.000 KWD.

## 3. Worked Quantity Estimate for a Kuwaiti Villa (قسيمة 400 متر)
- Ground plot: 400 m² (20m × 20m or 16m × 25m).
- 3 Floors (Basement/Ground + 1st + 2nd + Roof parapet).
- Gross facade area: 800 to 1,000 m².
- Deduct openings (windows/doors ~15%): Net area ≈ 700 to 850 m².
- Total Villa Finishing Cost in Sigma:
  - At 5.0 KWD/m² × 750 m² = **3,750 KWD**.
  - Compared to full stone cladding (15 KWD/m² × 750 m² = 11,250 KWD) -> **Saving 7,500 KWD!**

## 4. Key Failure Points in Kuwait's Heat & Dust
- **Poor base plaster (مساح تعبان ومفكك)**: If the underlying cement plaster is powdery or cracked, the Sigma will detach.
- **Cheap corner beads (زوايا صاج صيني رديء)**: They rust within two years and cause brown vertical rust stains across the facade.
- **Skipping the Topcoat (إلغاء طبقة الحماية الشفافة)**: Leaves the pores of the textured Sigma open to Kuwait's summer dust (الطوز), turning white/beige walls into dull brown within 12 months.
