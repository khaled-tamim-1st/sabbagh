# Research Dossier: ARTICLE-09 (أسباب انتفاخ دهان الطوفة وسقوط المعجون في البيوت الكويتية)

## 1. Executive Summary & Diagnostic Taxonomy
- **Search Intent**: Troubleshooting & Problem-Solving. Homeowners and tenants in Kuwait witnessing sudden paint blisters, peeling sheets, and hollow cracking putty on their walls.
- **Root Cause Categorization**:
  1. *Moisture Blistering (فقاعات مائية)*: Water vapor migrating from behind the wall (hidden AC drain leaks, bathroom tile grout degradation) and getting trapped under low-permeability latex/enamel films.
  2. *Dry/Adhesion Blistering (فقاعات جافة وتقشر هوائي)*: Loss of adhesion between the putty and masonry substrate, usually due to:
     - Skipping the penetrating primer (سيلر مائي).
     - Applying paint over dusty, unvacuumed sanding residue.
     - Applying water-based paint over glossy oil paint without scuff-sanding.
  3. *Alkaline Burn & Moisture Entrapment (حروق قلوية)*: Applying putty on fresh plaster before 28-day curing in new villas (المطلاع وغرب عبد الله المبارك).
  4. *Thermal Bridging & Condensation (نقطة الندى والتكثف الحراري)*: Interior condensation when cold AC air (20°C) hits poorly insulated exterior wall cores during Kuwait's August 90%+ humidity.

## 2. Professional Diagnostic Tests
- **The Coin/Knuckle Tap Test (اختبار التطبيل)**:
  - Solid dull thud = Sound structural bonding.
  - Hollow drum-like echo = Delaminated putty sheet that will collapse soon.
- **Cross-Hatch Adhesion Test (اختبار الالتصاق بالصليب والشريط)**:
  - Cutting an X with a utility knife and pulling duct tape; if paint flakes off, complete scraping is mandatory.

## 3. Remediation Protocol
1. Stop the root moisture cause (repair AC pipe or bathroom grout).
2. Mechanical scraping 15–20 cm beyond the affected perimeter until reaching solidly bonded edges.
3. Treat masonry with anti-fungal wash and deep penetrating primer (Jotun Barrier Primer / Caparol Tiefgrund).
4. Apply 2 thin coats of water-resistant acrylic/polymer putty with sanding between coats.
5. Apply matching topcoat with feathered edges to blend seamlessly into surrounding wall.
