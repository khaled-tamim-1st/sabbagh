## 1. Product Lines & Packaging in Kuwait
Jotun is the undisputed market leader in Kuwait's architectural coatings sector.
Packaging conventions in Kuwait:
1. **الربع جالون (1 لتر / 0.9 لتر)**: Used for color samples (تستر) or touch-ups.
2. **الجالون (4 لتر)**: Used for single rooms, feature accent walls, or bathrooms/kitchens.
3. **البستيل / الدرام الكبير (18 لتر / 16 لتر)**: Standard wholesale/contractor package for whole apartments and villas.

## 3. Why Prices Fluctuate: The "Base Type" Factor
Many Kuwaiti consumers don't realize that color tinting changes the price:
- **Base A (قاعدة فاتحة)**: Contains white pigment (Titanium Dioxide). Requires minimal tinting colorant. Lowest price.
- **Base B (قاعدة متوسطة)**: Medium tone. Slightly more expensive.
- **Base C (قاعدة داكنة / شفافة)**: Used for intense, dark shades (charcoal, navy, forest green). The heavy colorant concentrate adds 2 to 5 KWD per drum on the tinting machine.

## 4. Retail vs Authorized Distributors in Kuwait
- Primary wholesale/retail hub: **الشويخ الصناعية (شارع كندا دراي وشارع البنوك ومجمع جواد الصفار)**.
- Secondary hubs: **الفحيحيل الصناعية، الجهراء الحرفية، وأسواق القرين**.
- Crucial advice: Always inspect the batch date (تاريخ الإنتاج) stamped on the lid and make sure the factory plastic seal is intact.
