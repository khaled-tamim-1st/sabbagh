# Research Dossier: ARTICLE-07 (علاج رطوبة السرداب وتملح الجدران في الكويت)

## 1. Overview & Technical Diagnosis
- **Topic Context**: Kuwait villas often feature large basements (سراديب) utilized as diwaniyas, living spaces, storage, or master suites. Due to high groundwater tables in certain Kuwait residential areas (e.g., South Surra, Mishref, Farwaniya, Sabah Al-Ahmad) and failed exterior bitumastic roll insulation during construction, basements suffer from negative hydrostatic water ingress.
- **Physical Symptoms**:
  1. *تقشر دهان السرداب*: Paint bubbles, swells, and peels in broad sheets.
  2. *تملح الجدار (Efflorescence)*: Chalky white fuzzy powder erupts through plaster (أملاح بيضاء كيميائية ناتجة عن تبخر الرطوبة).
  3. *الرائحة الكريهة والعفن*: Musty basement odors caused by damp concrete, mold colonies, and inadequate subterranean ventilation.
  4. *تآكل أسفل النعلات وألواح الخشب*: Deterioration of wood skirting, doors, and drywall.

## 2. Engineering Remediation Protocol (Negative-Side Waterproofing)
Because excavating the outside of a finished villa is financially and structurally prohibitive in Kuwait, interior negative-side remediation is mandatory:
1. **Full Surface Strip**: Mechanical chipping of plaster and paint down to bare structural concrete block/retaining wall.
2. **Crack Chasing & Hydraulic Mortar**: V-cut cracks and joint interfaces; pack with rapid-setting hydraulic plug cement (مثل سيكا بلج Sika Plug أو ووتر ستوب).
3. **Crystalline Slurry Application**: 2 heavy coats of osmotic crystalline waterproofing (Penetron, Xypex, or SikaTop Seal-107). These chemically grow crystals deep into concrete pores.
4. **Breathable Anti-Salt Polymer Render**: Re-plastering with salt-resistant, breathable hydrophobic mortar.
5. **Vapor-Permeable Finishing Paint**: Interior painting with breathable silicate/mineral paints (دهانات تنفسية مسامية) that allow vapor transmission without trapping moisture or blistering.

## 3. Financial Benchmarks in Kuwait (2026 Data)
- **Point Injection (حقن الشروخ بمضخة الضغط العالي)**: 15 – 25 د.ك للباكر الواحد.
- **Negative-Side Cementitious Insulation (عزل أسمنتي سلبي مع كشط)**: 4.5 – 7.5 د.ك/م².
- **Sump Pit / Elevator Pit Waterproofing (عزل جورة السرداب أو بئر المصعد)**: 60 – 130 د.ك.
- **Full Basement Room Treatment (غرفة 20م² تشطيب شامل)**: 180 – 350 د.ك.
